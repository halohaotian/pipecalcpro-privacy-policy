PipeCalc Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Summary: PipeCalc Pro does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All calculations are performed entirely on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.

1. Overview

PipeCalc Pro ("the App") is a professional pipe sizing and plumbing calculator developed by Jianhui Wu ("the Developer"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information. PipeCalc Pro is a completely local, offline-capable calculator application. Specifically, the App:

  • Does not collect personal information (names, email addresses, phone numbers, etc.)
  • Does not collect device identifiers or usage data
  • Does not collect location data
  • Does not use cookies or any tracking technologies
  • Does not require user registration or account creation
  • Does not collect any input values or calculation results entered by the user

3. How the App Works

PipeCalc Pro performs all calculations locally on your device. The App includes:

  • Pipe Sizing Calculator: Recommends the smallest pipe size that can handle a given flow rate within a specified velocity limit, based on ANSI B36.10/B36.19 Schedule 40 and Schedule 80 pipe dimensions.
  • Flow Rate Calculator: Calculates flow rate in GPM (gallons per minute) based on pipe inner diameter and fluid velocity.
  • Cost Estimator: Estimates total material cost including a 15% waste allowance based on pipe length and unit price.

All calculations use built-in engineering formulas (simplified Hazen-Williams and Darcy-Weisbach) and pre-loaded pipe dimension data. No data is sent to any server or external service at any time.

4. Data Storage

The App does not store any data permanently. Calculation inputs are held only in temporary memory while you use the App and are discarded when you close it. The App does not use local databases, keychain storage, or any persistent storage mechanism.

5. Third-Party Services

PipeCalc Pro does not integrate with or use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • No advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • No analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel)
  • No crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • No social media SDKs or sharing features
  • No payment processors or in-app purchase systems
  • No cloud storage or syncing services

6. Network Access

The App does not require network access to function. All features work entirely offline. The App does not make any network requests to external servers.

7. Children's Privacy

PipeCalc Pro is a professional tool intended for engineers, plumbers, and contractors. While the App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13, it is not specifically directed at children and does not offer content targeted to children.

8. Permissions

PipeCalc Pro does not request any device permissions. The App does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Photo library
  • Location services
  • Contacts
  • Calendar or reminders
  • Bluetooth
  • Health or fitness data
  • HomeKit
  • Siri

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Since PipeCalc Pro does not collect or store any data, there is no data to retain or delete. Simply closing the App discards all temporary calculation inputs.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since no data is collected, changes to this policy will not affect any stored personal information.
